# SEBI BRSR Regulatory Framework & Core Attribute Mapping

This platform is structured in direct accordance with SEBI circular `SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122` dated July 12, 2023.

---

## 1. Top-Level BRSR Structure

### Section A: General Disclosures
- **I. Details of the Listed Entity**: Corporate name, CIN (`U45200TG2006PLC050277`), registered office, stock exchange listings, reporting currency (INR).
- **II. Products / Services**: Heavy civil infrastructure, EPC contracts, water lift irrigation systems, tunneling, and clean mobility EV buses.
- **III. Operations**: Number of locations where plants / operational sites exist (250+ project sites across India and abroad).
- **IV. Employees**: Total permanent employees, women participation percentage, contractual workforce.
- **V. Holding, Subsidiary and Associate Companies**: MEIL Energy Private Limited, MEIL Infrastructure Limited, Olectra Greentech Limited.
- **VI. CSR Details**: Mandatory 2% corporate social responsibility expenditures in infrastructure rehabilitation and local skills development.
- **VII. Transparency and Disclosures Compliances**: Grievance redressal mechanisms for communities, shareholders, and workers.

### Section B: Management and Process Disclosures
- Policy governance and oversight commitments across all 9 Business Responsibility Principles.
- Board committee oversight for ESG risks, physical climate risks, and regulatory adherence.

### Section C: Principle-Wise Performance Disclosures
- **Principle 1**: Businesses should conduct and govern themselves with integrity in a manner that is ethical, transparent, and accountable.
- **Principle 2**: Businesses should provide goods and services in a manner that is sustainable and safe throughout their life cycle.
- **Principle 3**: Businesses should respect and promote the well-being of all employees, including those in their value chains.
- **Principle 4**: Businesses should respect the interests of and be responsive to all stakeholders.
- **Principle 5**: Businesses should respect and promote human rights.
- **Principle 6**: Businesses should respect and make efforts to protect and restore the environment.
- **Principle 7**: Businesses, when engaging in influencing public and regulatory policy, should do so in a manner that is responsible and transparent.
- **Principle 8**: Businesses should promote inclusive growth and equitable development.
- **Principle 9**: Businesses should engage with and provide value to their consumers in a responsible manner.

## 2. Mandatory BRSR Core 9-Attribute Assurance Mapping

Under SEBI regulations, top listed entities must obtain **reasonable assurance** on the 9 BRSR Core attributes. The platform supports all 9 attributes natively:

| Attribute # | SEBI BRSR Core Attribute | Principle | Implemented Parameter in Platform | Primary Verification Evidence |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Green-house gas (GHG) footprint | P6 | Scope 1 (Direct Fuel) & Scope 2 (Grid Electricity) | IPCC factor logs & CEA grid certificates |
| **2** | Water footprint & intensity | P6 | Water withdrawal, consumption & recycling (KL) | Flowmeter telemetry & municipal water bills |
| **3** | Energy footprint & intensity | P6 | Total energy (MWh) and Renewable energy mix % | DISCOM bills & captive solar wheeling logs |
| **4** | Waste management & circularity | P6 | Total waste generated, recycled, and disposed (MT) | Hazardous waste manifest & recycler receipts |
| **5** | Employee well-being & safety | P3 | Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR) | Biometric logs & OHSAS safety registers |
| **6** | Gender diversity in workforce | P3 | Female participation in engineering & management | HR payroll database & PF return registers |
| **7** | Median wage ratios & fair pay | P3 | Minimum wage compliance & median wage ratios | Payroll compliance audit records |
| **8** | Value chain purchasing & vendor ESG | P2/P8 | Tier-1 vendor ESG audits & SME vendor spend % | SAP vendor management & procurement books |
| **9** | Openness of business & anti-corruption | P1 | Anti-corruption training & whistleblower cases | Compliance committee minutes & sign-offs |
