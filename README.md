## 🌟 1. Project Objective & Vision

**ESG360** is a centralized, intelligent, and audit-ready web-based ESG Reporting Platform engineered to help large conglomerates collect, validate, analyze, verify, and report Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) data according to **SEBI's BRSR and BRSR Core requirements**.

### Multi-Level Organizational Consolidation (Section 3):
$$\text{MEIL GROUP} \longrightarrow \text{SUBSIDIARY} \longrightarrow \text{BUSINESS UNIT} \longrightarrow \text{LOCATION / PROJECT}$$

The system automates bottom-up aggregation:
$$\text{Business Unit Level} \longrightarrow \text{Subsidiary Level} \longrightarrow \text{Group Level}$$

## 🎨 Design Aesthetics & Color Palette

- **Background**: Complete pitch black (`#000000` / `#050508`) with an active, ambient 60 FPS HTML5 canvas particle stream inspired by ChatGPT Astra.
- **Color Discipline**: **Strictly 0% Blue and 0% Green**. Replaced with an ultra-luxurious, developer-crafted palette:
  - **Solar Amber & Gold** (`#f59e0b`, `#fbbf24`, `#d97706`): Primary actions, verified badges, Scope 1 emissions, data quality indicators.
  - **Amethyst Violet & Purple** (`#a855f7`, `#c084fc`, `#9333ea`): Governance indicators, BRSR Core mappings, Scope 2 emissions, multi-period analytics.
  - **Sunset Rose & Coral** (`#f43f5e`, `#fb7185`): Anomaly detection warnings, critical alerts, audit discrepancy flags.
  - **Platinum / Titanium Zinc** (`#ffffff`, `#e4e4e7`, `#a1a1aa`, `#27272a`): High-contrast typography, frosted glass borders, monospace figures.
- **Multi-Device Responsiveness**: Handcrafted responsive CSS grid and flex layouts supporting:
  - **Laptops & Desktops** (1024px–1920px+): Full widescreen portal header, multi-column analytics, calculation simulators.
  - **Tablets** (768px–1024px): Adaptive side-by-side matrices and collapsible filters.
  - **Android Smartphones** (360px–412px, Samsung Galaxy, Pixel, etc.): Mobile hamburger drawer with full portal access, sticky bottom quick-action navigation dock, touch targets $\ge 44\text{px}$.

## 🏆 20. Hackathon Demo Flow (13 Steps)

You can launch the interactive guided walkthrough anytime by clicking **“Hackathon Demo Flow”** in the top navigation bar.

1. **Step 1**: Login as Business Unit Manager (`data.owner@meil.in` / `ravi@meil.in`).
2. **Step 2**: Enter ESG data (e.g., Diesel Consumption: 25,000 Litres).
3. **Step 3**: Upload supporting evidence (`Electricity_Bill_June.pdf`).
4. **Step 4**: Submit data for verification.
5. **Step 5**: System automatically calculates ESG metrics (Scope 1 = Fuel $\times 2.68$, Scope 2 = Electricity $\times 0.716$).
6. **Step 6**: System validates the submitted data across 7 validation rules.
7. **Step 7**: Anomaly warning triggers on July consumption spike (1,280 $\rightarrow$ 8,900 MWh) with `[Review Data]`, `[Accept]`, `[Request Correction]`.
8. **Step 8**: ESG Manager reviews and approves the data.
9. **Step 9**: Corporate dashboard automatically updates (Score 88/100, Data Quality 94/100).
10. **Step 10**: Open BRSR Mapping Engine.
11. **Step 11**: Review BRSR Principles (P1: 100%, P2: 85%, P3: 92%, P4: 100%) and BRSR Core KPIs.
12. **Step 12**: Ask the AI ESG Assistant natural language questions with source citations.
13. **Step 13**: Click **“GENERATE BRSR REPORT”** to produce the audit-ready SEBI filing.
